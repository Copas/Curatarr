Library

Custom posters

Pictures chosen for titles instead of Jellyfin's own: their posters, and their wide tiles, backgrounds, logos and banners. Upload one on a title's page, or drop pictures in an import folder. The picture each one replaced is kept, so it can be restored.

Import folder

Every couple of minutes Curatarr looks here for new pictures (JPEG, PNG or WebP) and puts each on the title its name matches: The Big Lebowski (1998).jpg, the-big-lebowski-1998.png or 08_the-big-lebowski.jpg. The year is needed only when titles share a name. A word before the extension makes it another of the title's pictures: ….thumb.jpg its wide tile, ….backdrop.jpg its background, ….logo.png its logo (a see-through PNG), ….banner.jpg its banner. A file saved again later replaces its picture. The folder must be readable by Curatarr's worker.

Without a picture of its own

Rows of wide tiles, a title's background and some apps' banners use pictures other than the poster. When an account sees its own poster (or a ribbon on one) but the title has no wide tile, background or banner made for it, show it one made from its poster, or Jellyfin's own. Needs the Curatarr plugin for Jellyfin (0.8.0.0 or later; 0.7.0.0 for wide tiles).

A logo is only ever one made for it.

Favourite ribbons

A gold tag on a title's poster saying whose favourite it is, so one account can see what another loves: "★ Sam" on the films Sam favourited, in every Jellyfin app. Needs the Curatarr plugin for Jellyfin. It's drawn over the poster each account would see anyway, its own custom poster and Leaving Soon's countdown included.

Make them with an AI

Curatarr writes the prompts; an image-making AI agent (Codex, or any other that can read and save files) makes the pictures and saves them in the import folder, where Curatarr puts them on. Each prompt is a batch, with each title's year, genres, plot and the file names to save them as. Asking for more than the poster gets a matching set: the same cast and style, each picture a different scene.
