Subtitles

Downloaded English subtitles are often timed for another release of the film, so lines turn up early or late. Curatarr listens to a few minutes of each film and matches what's said to the subtitle lines: ones off by a steady amount are re-timed (the original kept as “.unsynced”), and ones that can't be fixed that way are set aside (“.out-of-sync”) while Curatarr makes its own.
