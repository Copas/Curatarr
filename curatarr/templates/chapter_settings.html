Chapter generation{% if library %} · {{ library.name }}{% endif %}

Adds chapters to long movies and episodes that have none, after Sonarr or Radarr finish importing them. Chapters are written into MKV files in place: nothing is re-encoded, and files that already have chapters are left alone. Turning this on never processes your existing library; use Chapter maintenance for that.
