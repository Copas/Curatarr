Chapter generation{% if library %} · {{ library.name }}{% endif %}

Adds chapters to long movies and episodes that have none, after Sonarr or Radarr finish importing them. Chapters are written into MKV files in place: nothing is re-encoded, and files that already have chapters are left alone. Turning this on never processes your existing library; use Chapter maintenance for that.

A ceiling: Curatarr starts fewer whenever the machine or someone watching needs the room. Only one job transcribes at a time; the rest scan scenes, read files or wait on the language model.
Lower finds more, subtler changes.
Experimental. Flags an episode whose transcript fits another episode's title and summary in Sonarr far better than its own (a misfiled download), or whose default audio isn't English. It finds real mix-ups, but with vague summaries it also flags episodes that are fine, so it's off by default.
Intro Skipper's "recaps" and "intros" on movies come from chapter names like "Chapter 1"; skip-marker checks hide them (each listed on Review skip markers, with Undo). Credits stay, for Skip Credits and Next.
Curatarr adjusts how many jobs run from CPU and GPU use (including Jellyfin's video decoding), who's watching, and whether Jellyfin is transcoding: it adds jobs while there's room, holds back as the machine fills, and pauses running jobs if playback needs the room. Off, it ignores playback and only watches the load.
Only needed when Sonarr/Radarr see the files at a different path than Curatarr does (for example in Docker).
