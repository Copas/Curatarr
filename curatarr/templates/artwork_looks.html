When the image service refuses a picture

ChatGPT's safety system sometimes turns a picture down, usually a trademarked character (a superhero's costume and emblem) with real people's faces in it. Asked the same way it's refused again, so Curatarr tries it once more as an original take: a new scene, without the title's own art, with this note added. If that's turned down too, it stops; a local engine, or the title's own art, is left to you. Pictures made this way are marked in Review.
