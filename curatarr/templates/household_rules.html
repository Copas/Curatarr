{% call card('picks', 'star', 'Picks for each person', ((picks|selectattr('picks')|list|length) ~ ' playlist' ~ ('s' if (picks|selectattr('picks')|list|length) != 1 else '') ~ ' of ten films, made again weekly') if options.picks else 'A playlist of films for each account', options.picks, open=options.picks, toggles='picks') %}

A private Jellyfin playlist for each account, "Picks for" their name, of ten unwatched films matched to what they've favourited and finished. It shows on every Jellyfin app, Roku included, under Playlists. Only films that account can see (its libraries and parental rating) are picked. A film comes off the list a few minutes after it's watched, and the list is made again each week. An account needs five favourited or finished films first.

{{ switch('picks', 'Make picks for each person', options.picks, "How films are chosen: each film is described by its genres and TMDB keywords, rarer ones counting for more. No single liked film explains more than two picks and no genre takes more than four places, so one much-loved film can't fill the list. A pick left unwatched counts for less each week, so others get a turn. Picks never include films that person has watched or started.") }}
